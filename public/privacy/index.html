1. Who we are

We are Alchemists.AI Private Limited (CIN U62090KA2025PTC203883), Bengaluru, India ("we", "us"). EasyDocument.ai, formerly DocInflow, is our product brand and is not a separate company. We also provide managed services and product development to businesses, through alchemists.ltd. We are the developer named on the App Store and Google Play listings.

EasyDocument.ai is a business tool. Organisations use it to read, check and manage trade, finance and contract documents.

  • Data about you, such as your account, enquiries, billing details or job application: we decide how it is used, so we act as the Data Fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
  • Documents and data an organisation gives us, whether uploaded to EasyDocument.ai or shared with us for managed services or product development: we process them for that organisation, on its instructions. The organisation is the Data Fiduciary for that data, and our agreement with it governs that processing.

3. How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To read, extract, check and organise the documents you or your organisation submit, which is the service itself.
  • To keep the audit trail your organisation relies on.
  • To answer enquiries, scope and deliver managed services and product development work, and manage customers, clients and vendors.
  • To invoice, collect payment and keep the tax and statutory records the law requires.
  • To send service emails: invitations, password resets and alerts about your documents.
  • To send occasional product news, only if you agree. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To assess job applications.
  • To keep our services running, fix faults and prevent misuse.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not use your data for advertising. We do not sell it.

We process your personal data with your consent and for the legitimate uses the DPDP Act allows. We process the documents an organisation gives us on that organisation's instructions.

4. AI processing

To read a document, we send its content to an AI model provider, listed in section 5. The provider returns the extracted results, and we store them in India. Before your first upload, the app tells you this and asks you to agree.

  • No training, ever. We never use your documents or extracted data to train or fine-tune any model, ours or anyone else's. Our providers are contractually barred from doing so. This has no opt-in.
  • Providers use the content only to return results to us, and delete it under the terms of their contract with us.
  • AI output can be wrong. The app shows what it found and leaves the decision to a person in your organisation.

6. Where your data is stored

We store your data in India, in Google Cloud's Mumbai region. This covers the database, uploaded files, backups and our logs.

When a document is read, its content is sent to an AI model provider (section 5), which may process it outside India. The provider keeps it only as long as its contract with us allows. We do not transfer data to any country the Government of India has restricted.

8. Deleting your account

EasyDocument.ai accounts are created for you by your organisation. To delete your account, ask your organisation's administrator, or write to privacy@easydocument.ai. We delete your account details within 30 days of the request.

Documents you uploaded belong to your organisation's workspace. Deleting your account does not delete them. Your organisation's administrator controls them, or ask us and we will pass the request on. Your name stays in activity records your organisation must keep, but nowhere else.

9. Managed services and product development

When we deliver managed services or product development, the client decides what data we receive and how it is used. We process it only for the work described in our signed agreement with that client, which governs that processing, and the no-training commitment in section 4 applies to it too. If your details reached us through one of our clients, please contact that client first. We will help them respond.

10. Job applicants

We use your application to assess your fit for a role and to contact you. With your consent we may keep it for future roles, for up to 12 months. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

11. Cookies

Our websites use only the cookies they need to work, such as keeping you signed in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. You can block cookies in your browser settings, but parts of the sites may then stop working. Some pages load fonts or code from third-party content networks, which receive your IP address to deliver them.

12. Security

  • Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest.
  • Credentials for systems you connect, such as SAP, are encrypted separately with a key held outside the database.
  • Role-based access: people see only what their role allows.
  • Production access is limited to named staff, and an audit trail records changes.
  • Separate development, staging and production environments.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will tell you, your organisation and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires. To report a security issue, write to security@easydocument.ai.

13. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:

  • ask what personal data we hold about you and who we have shared it with;
  • correct or update it;
  • ask us to erase it, unless we must keep it by law;
  • withdraw consent, as easily as you gave it;
  • name someone to act for you if you die or cannot act yourself; and
  • complain to our Grievance Officer, and then to the Data Protection Board of India.

For documents your organisation uploaded, send your request to your organisation first, since it decides about that data. We will help it respond.

Write to privacy@easydocument.ai. We may ask you to confirm your identity. We will reply within 15 days.

14. Children

Our services and websites are for business use by adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we learn we have, we delete it.

15. Changes to this policy

We will post any change here and update the effective date. If a change materially affects how we use your data, we will tell you in the app, by email or on our websites before it takes effect.
